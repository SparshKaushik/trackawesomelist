Projects

Automation, Manufacturing, and Robotics

  • FarmBot - Automated gardening machine to grow vegetables.
  • Hacker Fab - Nanofabrication tools for making integrated circuits.
  • JPL Open Source Rover (⭐9.6k) - A six wheeled, build-it-yourself, open-source rover.
  • Mekanika - Tools and machines for makers.
  • Millennium Machines - A project dedicated to designing desktop CNC mills.
  • Open Source Ecology - Industrial machines made open.
  • Opulo - Project that develops a pick and place machine.
  • Precious Plastic - Tools to make plastic recycling as simple as possible.
  • Prusa3D - 3D printer manufacturer who shares their work open online.
  • RepRap - Humanity's first general-purpose, self-replicating manufacturing machine.
  • VORON Design - No-compromise 3D printer designs.
  • WikiHouse - Digital designed open housing.

Consumer Devices

  • freeDSP - A family of audio digital signal processors.
  • Input Labs - Video game controllers featuring gyroscopic sensors.
  • Mutable Instruments - Eurorack classics.
  • PiKVM - Inexpensive DIY IP-KVM based on Raspberry Pi.
  • PiSpot Show (⭐3) - Raspberry Pi appliance that drives HDMI displays as self-updating Wi-Fi voucher kiosks with live weather.
  • PiSpot Watch (⭐3) - Wrist-wearable Raspberry Pi Zero smartwatch with e-ink display that generates Wi-Fi voucher codes on demand.
  • Ploopy - 3D-printable peripherals running the QMK firmware.
  • SlimeVR - Full-body tracking sensors for virtual reality and motion capture.
  • Winterbloom - Eurorack modules; Thea has some stellar design write-ups on her blog.

Development Boards

  • Arduino - Arduino is an electronics platform.
  • iCEBreaker - Low-cost FPGA development board based around the Lattice iCE40.
  • Icepi Zero (⭐819) - A Lattice ECP5-based FPGA development board in the form factor of the Raspberry Pi Zero.
  • nRF52840 ProMicro (⭐92) - A Nordic nRF52840-based development board in the form factor of the Arduino Pro Micro.
  • OpenMV (⭐2.9k) - Low-cost machine vision platform.
  • pic0rick (⭐99) - A Raspberry Pi Pico platform for pulse-echo ultrasound imaging hardware.

Scientific Instruments

  • Biohack Academy - Biotechnological hardware such as an incubator, thermocycler, centrifuge, microscope, etc.
  • GaudiLab - Hardware projects for biology laboratories.
  • HackRF Pro (⭐51) - Upgraded version of the HackRF One (⭐8.1k), a low-cost software-defined radio.
  • LibreVNA (⭐1.6k) - A vector network analyzer capable of measurements up to 6 GHz.
  • mesoSPIM - Light-sheet microscopes for imaging in cleared tissue.
  • Open Gamma Detector (⭐366) - Hackable, low-cost gamma-ray spectrometer.
  • OpenBCI - Brain-computer interface.
  • OpenFlexure - 3D-printed microscope with a precise mechanical stage.
  • OpenSPIM - Open-access platform for three-dimensional (3D) microscopy.
  • openUC2 (⭐531) - Modular microscopy toolbox.
  • SafeCast - Environmental measurements for the public domain.

Design Tools

Mechanical and Electrical

  • Elmer (⭐1.7k) - A software suite for running finite element method simulations.
  • FreeCAD - A mechanical computer-aided design program for designing 3D parametric parts.
  • Jumpstarter (⭐211) - Open source hardware-in-the-loop testing framework for automated testing on real and virtual hardware with CI/CD integration.
  • KiCad - An electronic design automation program for designing printed circuit boards.
  • KiCad StepUp (⭐682) - A mechanical collaboration tool for moving projects between KiCad and FreeCAD.
  • Ngspice - A mixed-signal electronic circuit simulator.

Integrated Circuit

  • KLayout - A tool for viewing and editing integrated circuit layouts.
  • LibreLane - An infrastructure library for creating integrated circuit design flows.
  • Magic - A VLSI layout tool, primarily used for analog layout.
  • OpenRAM - A framework for creating SRAM netlists, layouts, and more.
  • OpenROAD - An application that implements an RTL-to-GDSII flow.
  • SkyWater PDK (⭐3.7k) - A process design kit for SkyWater's 130 nm process node.
  • Surfer - An extensible and snappy waveform viewer.
  • Verilator - A program for simulating HDL code.
  • Yosys - A framework for RTL synthesis.

Conferences

  • FOSDEM - Annual open source event in Brussels.
  • Open Hardware Summit - Annual conference on open hardware.
  • Open Sauce - Annual convention focused on science and technology on on 17-19 July
  • Maker Faire - A celebration of the Maker Movement, locally organized.
  • Hackaday Supercon - Annual conference on hardware hacking and deep-dive technical workshops
  • KiCon - Annual conferences about KiCad open source EDA organized in different regions
  • RISC-V Summits - Annual summits organized by RISC-V International in different regions
  • World RISC-V Days - Synchronized global events voluntarily organized by RISC-V communities worldwide
  • Latch-Up - A three-day conference dedicated to free and open source silicon. Hosted in North America every spring.
  • ORConf - Another three-day conference dedicated to free and open source silicon. Hosted in Europe every autumn.

Platforms

  • Open Hardware Repository - For electronics designers at experimental physics facilities to collaborate on open hardware designs.
  • Open Hardware Observatory - Free blueprints for sustainable open hardware.
  • Instructables - Instructables is a place that lets you explore, document, and share your creations.
  • Hackaday.io - Hackaday.io is the world's largest collaborative hardware development community.
  • Hackster.io - Connecting developers where exploring tomorrow starts today.
  • Openhardware.io - Tracking amazing open hardware projects.
  • Openhardware.science - Make open science hardware ubiquitous by 2025.
  • Openlifescience - Program to make Open Science ambassadors in research.
  • Thingiverse - A platform for sharing and contributing to design hardware for 3D printing, laser cutting and CNC milling.
  • Kitspace.org - A place to build and share electronics projects.
  • BoardRepo - Share KiCad and Altium projects as a link, with schematics, PCB views, BOM, and gerbers rendered in the browser.
