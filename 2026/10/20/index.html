  • Device Changer - Applies device models, build fingerprints and identifiers to rooted Android test devices to reproduce device-specific bugs.

Software / Test Data Management

  • TempMail Hub - Free disposable inbox for manual or scripted signup and email-verification testing, with a selectable lifetime from 10 minutes to 7 days and no account required.
  • ByteRivet Sample Files - Free browser-based generator for exact-size dummy files from 1 KB to 2 GB in 22 formats (PDF, PNG, JPG, DOCX, XLSX, CSV, JSON, ZIP, MP4, WAV and more) for upload-limit and file-handling tests. Files are built locally, no upload or signup.
