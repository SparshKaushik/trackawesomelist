20. Awesome Billing

Marketplace / Market Research

  • “If we only paid in $0.07 intervals, their commission algorithm would round down to the nearest whole cent (…) on the unit transaction level, not the monthly invoice level.” (source) - Mechanical Turk's rake was “20% (…) on the reward and bonus amount”, with a minimum fee of $0.01 per bonus payment, so a 7-cent bonus is billed one cent instead of 1.4: an effective 14.3% instead of 20%. The requester's round_payment() snaps every payout onto the 5-cent grid where that truncation always applies, and holds anything below 7 cents in the worker's wallet: “~$50 a day” saved. Rounding per transaction rather than per invoice hands the remainder to whoever picks the transaction amounts.
